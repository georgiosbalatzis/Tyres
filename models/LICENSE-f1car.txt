A generic single-seater: no team livery, logos or textures.

Changes made for F1 Stories — Tyre Intelligence:
- Taken from the F1 Stories ghostcar project (same file), with EXT_meshopt_compression decoded so the
  browser needs no WebAssembly decoder (the site's CSP does not allow one). Geometry is otherwise unchanged.
- At runtime (src/three/modelLoader.ts) the body is recoloured graphite/carbon and the model's tyres are
  hidden and replaced by procedural wheels that carry the derived tyre-demand colouring.
